Research Overview

My research focuses on the integration of remote sensing, geospatial analytics, and artificial intelligence for precision agriculture and environmental monitoring. I work with satellite, airborne, UAV, and in-situ multispectral and hyperspectral data to analyze land, crop, and ecosystem conditions with greater accuracy and insight.

The broader goal of my work is to develop data-driven approaches that support sustainable resource management, improve decision making in agriculture, and advance the practical use of AI in geospatial and Earth observation applications.

Research Interests

  • Precision agriculture and crop monitoring
  • Remote sensing and geospatial data analysis
  • Multispectral and hyperspectral image processing
  • Artificial intelligence for Earth observation

Weather AI and Earth Observation

I am also interested in emerging AI approaches for weather forecasting and climate-related decision making. Google DeepMind's WeatherNext 3 combines live satellite observations with machine learning to produce hourly, high-resolution forecasts, including detailed predictions for temperature, precipitation, wind, and renewable-energy applications. These developments illustrate how Earth observation data and AI can support more timely and localized environmental intelligence.

The following resources provide further information and examples:

  • Weather Lab, an interactive platform for exploring live forecast layers, tracking tropical cyclones, and comparing experimental weather AI models with traditional meteorological baselines.
  • Introducing WeatherNext 3, the Google DeepMind article describing the model's satellite data, increased resolution, precipitation forecasting, and practical applications.
  • WeatherNext, an overview of Google's global AI weather forecasting models and their applications.

Weather Lab is an experimental research platform. For official forecasts, warnings, and public-safety guidance, refer to the relevant national or local meteorological agency.
