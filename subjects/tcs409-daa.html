TCS409 - Design and Analysis of Algorithms

This course introduces the fundamental concepts of algorithm design, analysis, and optimization. It helps students understand how to solve computational problems efficiently and how to choose appropriate techniques based on time, space, and scalability requirements.

The course builds a strong foundation for problem solving, coding interviews, and advanced study in computer science.

What you will learn

  • Algorithm design strategies such as divide and conquer, greedy, and dynamic programming
  • Analysis of time and space complexity
  • Sorting, searching, and graph algorithms
  • Approaches to proving correctness and comparing algorithm performance

Key topics

  • Asymptotic notation and complexity analysis
  • Recurrence relations and algorithm efficiency
  • Greedy algorithms and their limitations
  • Dynamic programming and backtracking
  • Graph traversal, shortest path, and spanning trees
