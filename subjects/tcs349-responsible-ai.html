TCS349 - Responsible AI

Responsible AI focuses on making artificial intelligence fair, safe, transparent, and accountable, while Explainable AI helps people understand how and why an AI system makes a decision. Both ideas are important because they build trust and ensure that AI is used in a way that benefits society.
